Ambulance and emergency transport

Bringing a patient in? Tell us three things.

Our ambulance runs around the clock. Calling ahead means the emergency team is ready when you arrive — and someone can talk you through what to do while you wait.

Call +251 111 132 702

One number for ambulance dispatch and emergency advice, day or night.

  1. Where you are Your location and the nearest landmark — a road name, building, church or school we can find quickly.
  2. How many patients How many people need help, and whether anyone is a child, pregnant or badly injured.
  3. How to reach you Access notes: floor, gate, security, a narrow road or heavy traffic — and a number we can call back.

Do not move someone who may have a spinal injury. Call first and stay with the patient. If their condition changes, call back — the line is answered day and night.
