Our story

Built for the way families in Lemi Kura live.

Summit General Hospital was built to bring dependable, multi-specialty care closer to the families of Lemi Kura. Our campus at Summit 72 brings outpatient clinics, emergency care, maternity, surgery and diagnostics together — so patients don't have to travel across the city for coordinated care.

Two ideas shape how we work. Medicine is a conversation: we explain what we know, what we don't, and what comes next. And care is a team effort: our consultants, nurses, pharmacists and technicians share one plan for you.

Today we serve the neighbourhoods around Summit, Lemi Kura and CMC — people of every age, background and language. Our promise is simple: careful medicine, clearly explained, close to home.
