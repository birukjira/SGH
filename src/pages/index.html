Welcome to Summit

A general hospital, built around the person.

At Summit General Hospital, good care starts with listening. Our consultants, nurses and support teams work together under one roof — so your questions are answered, your options are clear, and your family stays close.

From our emergency department to outpatient clinics, diagnostics and operating theatres, we bring dependable care to the Summit 72 community and beyond.

  • Consultant-led care across 15+ specialties
  • Laboratory, imaging and pharmacy on site
  • Care in Amharic, English and Afaan Oromo
